# Cinematic long takes

Cinematic mood over information: longer takes, one consistent grade, sparse lowercase captions, and an opening line chosen because it's intriguing, not because it explains.

- Aim for 20-45 s unless the brief says otherwise. Open on an evocative or philosophical line, even if it raises a question the clip only half answers. Hook text is short and lowercase, or empty.
- Let it breathe: 2-4 segments of 5-10 s. Keep a pause before the key line instead of cutting it, but cut any pause that would read as dead air.
- Captions: plain, caption_words 2-4, caption_size small, caption_color white, caption_position middle, caption_caps false. caption_position upper only when there is no hook; caption_style none is allowed when the speech is clear.
- Framing: crop with an off-centre composition (crop_x 0.35-0.65 around the face) or fit for a wide shot; zoom 1.2-1.5 for an intimate close-up.
- color_filter film or bw. No punch-ins. speed 1.0: the mood comes from long takes and the grade, not from slowed speech.
- End on a line that lingers.
