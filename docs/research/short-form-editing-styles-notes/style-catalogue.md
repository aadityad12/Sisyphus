# Named short-form vertical video editing styles (2025-2026)

Method note: ~20 searches and fetches. Almost every source is a vendor blog (Submagic, SendShort, Blitzcut, Opus), a freelancer listing, or an SEO page. No source gave frame-level measurements for any named creator except Tubefilter's MrBeast cut count (long-form, 2024). Numbers below are therefore tool presets and rules of thumb, not measured ground truth. Anything under "Inferences" is my synthesis, partly from general knowledge, and is labelled as such.

## Which named styles exist, and which are real?

### Takeaway
Real, widely named styles: Hormozi (caption package), MrBeast, Ali Abdaal, Iman Gadzhi, the Diary-of-a-CEO podcast clip, Vox/video-essay, green-screen explainer, brain rot, lo-fi/UGC/talking-head static, documentary/cinematic. Vendors (Submagic, Opus, Kapwing, SendShort, Choppity) sell presets named for Hormozi, MrBeast, Ali Abdaal, Iman Gadzhi, Devin Jatho, Think Media and Jon Youshaei. "Beast" as a caption preset name was not found; "MrBeast" was.

### Cited Findings
- Submagic's library includes Ali Abdaal, Devin Jatho, Alex Hormozi, MrBeast and Iman Gadzhi caption templates; it added four new Hormozi styles in 2024 — [Submagic: Ali Abdaal captions](https://www.submagic.co/blog/make-captions-like-ali-abdaal) (as relayed by search snippet; page itself 404'd on direct fetch)
- Opus Clip advertises one-click caption styles "from creators such as Ali Abdaal, Think Media, and Jon Youshaei"; no official template-name list found — [OpusClip Captions](https://www.opus.pro/tools/opusclip-captions)
- Opus's 2026 list of 11 aesthetics "showing up most consistently in viral 2026 clips": Brainrot stack, Vox/video essay, Talking-head static, UGC review, POV scenario, Hands-only ASMR, Audio-reactive AI, Aesthetic flat-lay, Documentary realism, Greenscreen explain, Phone-first cinematic — [Opus: 11 editing aesthetics](https://www.opus.pro/blog/editing-aesthetics-dominating-short-form-2026)
- CapCut's trends page lists beat-synced music edits, glitch transitions, mini-vlog storytelling, and Y2K / "clean girl" looks built on specific filters, colour tones and minimal transitions — [CapCut trends](https://www.capcut.com/help/latest-capcut-editing-trends) (via search snippet)
- "Italian brainrot" is a sub-genre of brain rot using exaggerated AI characters with Italian-sounding names; Oxford named "brain rot" 2024 Word of the Year — [search results, low-quality hosts](https://vidmuse.ai/blog/how-to-make-ai-brainrot-video)

### Inferences
- Of Opus's 11, the ones usable on talking-head/podcast/talk footage with no new shooting: Brainrot stack (as a gameplay split-screen), Vox/video essay (needs B-roll/graphics we may not have), Talking-head static, Greenscreen explain (presenter in front of an image), Documentary realism (grade + slow zoom), Phone-first cinematic (grade only). UGC review, POV, ASMR, flat-lay, audio-reactive AI need footage we cannot shoot.
- Suggested catalogue of 12 (see Style cards below), each tied to a source or flagged as unsourced.

### Gaps
- No source defines "storytime", "listicle/countdown", "news/commentary reaction" or "minimalist vlog" with numbers. Not researched to depth.
- Reddit r/VideoEditing discussion was not retrieved; search tool did not surface it.

## Per-style traits (length, cuts, captions, zoom, grade, audio, hook, ending, framing)

### Takeaway
Only captions and cut pace have any quantitative backing. Zoom amount, grade, music, hook and ending specifics are mostly unsourced; use the Inferences as editable defaults, not facts.

### Cited Findings

**Hormozi captions**
- Font: Montserrat Black (900) or Anton are the closest free matches; older style used "TheBoldFont", newer uses Anton — [Submagic](https://www.submagic.co/blog/how-to-make-alex-hormozi-captions); search summary of tool guides: [Hormozi caption search](https://www.submagic.co/pl/blog/how-to-make-alex-hormozi-captions)
- Uppercase, slight shadow, newer variant has yellow stroke, pop-in from bottom to top; emojis animated between lines; light-flare transitions and B-roll "show don't tell" — [Submagic](https://www.submagic.co/blog/how-to-make-alex-hormozi-captions)
- Words per caption: 2-4 (Mosaic API default 2-4; CapCut tutorial 2-3; Submagic CapCut setup max 4-6 words over 2 lines; Premiere setup max 15 chars/line, min 2 s caption) — [Mosaic docs](https://docs.mosaic.so/tiles/captions.md), [Submagic](https://www.submagic.co/blog/how-to-make-alex-hormozi-captions)
- Size: one preset 7.2% of video height; a 1080x1920 guide suggests 48-56 px (numbers disagree) — [Lilys CapCut note](https://lilys.ai/en/notes/create-youtube-shorts-20260115/capcut-hormozi-captions)
- Full package per Blitzcut: Montserrat Black or Proxima Nova Bold, white ALL CAPS, yellow `#f7c204` keyword, "woosh" on word entry, emoji between lines — [Blitzcut 2026 caption trends](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/tiktok-caption-trends-2026)
- Hook: opens with "a really compelling statement"; editing described as ruthless removal of digressions; seated talking head, first person — [Riverside](https://riverside.com/blog/hormozi-style-videos)

**MrBeast**
- Cut counts, one minute from the midpoint: 38 cuts (2023 "Real Assassin" video) vs 23 cuts (later "Abandoned City") i.e. one cut per ~1.6 s down to ~2.6 s; these are long-form, not Shorts. MrBeast said in March 2024 he slowed pacing and urged others to stop over-fast editing — [Tubefilter](https://tubefilter.com/2024/03/04/mrbeast-editing-style-number-of-cuts-per-video/)
- Text: Bebas Neue / Arial Black bold overlays, jump cuts at key moments, high-contrast saturated colour (SendShort, vendor, no numbers) — [SendShort MrBeast](https://sendshort.ai/guides/mrbeast-style/)

**Ali Abdaal**
- Word-by-word captions with colour change; handwritten-style look reported; one tool describes a black-and-white grade with colour pops, another a white/grey/black/brown/royal-blue palette (conflict) — [Submagic](https://www.submagic.co/blog/make-captions-like-ali-abdaal), [Choppity](https://www.choppity.com/tools/recreate-video-editing-style/ali-abdaal/)
- Jump cuts remove pauses; heavy custom B-roll visualising abstract ideas — [Adobe case study](https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2024/09/05/how-ai-helps-ali-abdaal-save-hours-every-week)

**Iman Gadzhi**
- Only qualitative: fast cuts, bold Montserrat overlays, subtle/dynamic zooms on key moments, speed ramps, LUT for consistent colour, SFX on transitions, simple cuts/fades over elaborate transitions, 9:16 — [SendShort Iman Gadzhi](https://sendshort.ai/guides/iman-gadzhi-style/). Another summary mentions high-end minimalist animations and 3D scenes — [search result](https://sozai.app/transcript/ai-edit-iman-gadzhi-videos/)

**Diary of a CEO / podcast clips**
- Clips 45-75 s; clip ~1/3 the length of its source segment; low orchestral sting under nearly every clip; captions because most TikTok views are muted — [PlayPodcasts](https://playpodcasts.com/blog/cut-for-the-algorithm-british-podcasts-short-form-clips) (via search summary; direct fetch 404'd)
- Long-form intros cut roughly every 2.5-3 s except for emotional beats (informal estimate, not Shorts) — [A Memo to Editors](https://amemotoeditors.beehiiv.com/p/4-ways-the-diary-of-a-ceo-hooks-their-audience)
- Generic podcast clip practice: cut to whoever is speaking, punch-in to emphasise a key line, break captions at natural phrase units — [CapCut podcast clips](https://www.capcut.com/create/podcast-clips-for-social-media-long-form-editing)

**Opus table (2026, rows with numbers)**
- Brainrot stack: cuts every 0.6-0.8 s, 2-3 stacked layers, white captions with colour pop, gameplay + ASMR layer
- Vox/video essay: chart or map every 8-10 s, one sans-serif, lower-thirds, music low, voiceover forward
- Talking-head static: single shot, single angle, no cuts, captions slightly off-centre, hard-cut paragraphs
- Greenscreen explain: visual swapped every 4-6 s, presenter in one third of frame, clean VO, minimal music
- Documentary realism: one grade throughout, slow zooms, 24 fps feel, minimal text
- Phone-first cinematic: one LUT, light grain, slow gimbal moves
— all [Opus 2026](https://www.opus.pro/blog/editing-aesthetics-dominating-short-form-2026)

**General pacing**
- Rule of thumb: a visual change every 1.5-2 s for videos under 60 s (2026 vendor article) — [Aibrify](https://aibrify.com/en/blog/short-form-video-editing-captions-b-roll-guide) (via search snippet)

**Lo-fi / anti-aesthetic**
- Handheld, natural light, unscripted, grain/glitch, minimal edit; "73% of creators add imperfections" and "higher watch-through" are unverified claims — [PodcastVideos.com, 2026-02-28](https://www.podcastvideos.com/articles/rise-of-anti-aesthetic-lo-fi-video-content-trends-2026)

### Inferences (my synthesis, partly unsourced; treat as defaults to tune)
Proposed style files, with talking-head fit (T=strong, P=possible, N=needs footage we lack):
1. **Hormozi captions** (T): 2-4 word uppercase Montserrat/Anton, white + yellow keyword, bottom-third or centre, 1 punch-in per 3-5 s, 20-45 s, hook = strongest sentence first.
2. **Podcast clip / DOAC** (T): 30-75 s, speaker-switch cuts on two-cam or reframed single-cam, cut every 3-5 s, slow punch-ins on emphasis, clean 2-line captions, low sting/bed music, ends on punchline or cliffhanger.
3. **Ali Abdaal talking-head + text** (T): jump-cut silence removal, restrained word-by-word captions with colour change, B-roll/graphics only if available, cut every 2-4 s.
4. **Iman Gadzhi luxury-motivational** (P, needs B-roll): tight cuts, Montserrat overlays, zooms, speed ramps, one LUT, SFX on cuts, driving music.
5. **MrBeast-pace** (P): cut every ~1.5-2.5 s, saturated grade, bold overlays. Note creator himself moved away from it.
6. **Brain rot / stacked** (P): talking head on top, gameplay or looping footage below (gameplay must be a stock asset), cuts every 0.6-0.8 s per Opus, constant SFX, 20-40 s.
7. **Static talking head** (T): one shot, minimal cuts, captions, hard-cut paragraphs; cheapest to implement.
8. **Green-screen explainer** (P): presenter cut-out small, screenshot/slide background swapped every 4-6 s; needs source images.
9. **Vox/video-essay** (N/P): needs charts/maps every 8-10 s; only works with generated graphics.
10. **Documentary/cinematic** (T for grade): single LUT, grain, slow push-in, sparse lowercase captions, no emoji, music bed low.
11. **Quiet-aesthetic minimal** (T): lowercase thin serif/sans captions (Poppins Light, DM Serif Display, Georgia), white or butter yellow `#F5D76E`, centred, single line, no animation, no SFX — per Blitzcut.
12. **Lo-fi authentic** (T): no grade, simple captions or none, hard cuts only at pauses, no music.
13. **Colored pill/chip captions** and **typewriter reveal** as caption variants that can attach to any of the above.
Zoom defaults (unsourced): punch-in scale 105-115% over 0.2-0.4 s on hard cuts; alternating 100/110% on successive cuts is a common trick to disguise jump cuts.

### Gaps
- No measured cut rates for Hormozi, Gadzhi, Abdaal, Flagrant, Rogan clips. Needs direct frame analysis of sample clips.
- No sourced zoom magnitudes, LUT values, music BPM/loudness, or ending patterns for any style.
- Storytime, listicle/countdown, news/reaction, split-screen gameplay: no usable sources. No study of split-screen gameplay and retention exists in what I found.

## Trending vs dated

### Takeaway
The full Hormozi package is described as saturated (baseline, not differentiating). Word-by-word karaoke captions are now standard. Rising: understated captions ("Dynamic Minimalism"), pill backgrounds, quiet-aesthetic lowercase, plus anti-polish/lo-fi and documentary realism as two opposing counter-trends. Evidence is vendor commentary only.

### Cited Findings
- Hormozi package "saturated"; shake/vibrate per word (2022-23) now overused; static full-sentence blocks look low-effort; neon green/electric blue fading; 3+ colours per line out; script/thin fonts out for primary captions; Dynamic Minimalism ("new meta") = heavy-weight white text, black outline, 2-3 words, subtle scale/fade, no SFX — [Blitzcut 2026](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/tiktok-caption-trends-2026)
- 2023 newsletter claimed the Hormozi look was "dead" because Fiverr sellers saturated it, and that Hormozi dropped it (unverified) — [Video Builders](https://videobuilders.beehiiv.com/p/hormozi-captions)
- Unlinked claim that 2025 A/B tests show overuse lowers trust with business audiences — [FluxNote 2026](https://fluxnote.io/guides/hormozi-style-captions-tutorial-guide-2026), unverified
- Talking-head static called the aesthetic behind "dozens of $1M-ARR creators in 2026"; documentary realism a counter-trend that stands out — [Opus](https://www.opus.pro/blog/editing-aesthetics-dominating-short-form-2026)
- Lo-fi rising (Artlist, eMarketer) vs. claim that "lo-fi slop" is ending in favour of high-production looks (Stormy.ai) — [Artlist](https://artlist.io/blog/authentic-content/), [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/video-advertising-native-tiktok), [Stormy.ai](https://stormy.ai/blog/rise-of-cinematic-content-aesthetics-growth-lever)
- MrBeast publicly rejected hyper-fast pacing in March 2024 — [Tubefilter](https://tubefilter.com/2024/03/04/mrbeast-editing-style-number-of-cuts-per-video/)

### Inferences
- Keep the Hormozi file but make the highlight colour and SFX optional and offer a "Dynamic Minimalism" variant as the modern default.
- Ship both a polished/cinematic and a lo-fi style since sources disagree on direction.

### Gaps
- No platform-published data (TikTok/Meta/YouTube) on which styles perform; all trend claims are vendor-authored.
