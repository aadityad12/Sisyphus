# Brain rot jump cuts

A good clip in this style never lets attention drop: the picture changes every one and a half to three seconds, and the captions are big enough to read with the sound off.

- Fast cuts, jump zooms and frantic pacing are the point here: do not send it back for pacing that is too fast.
- Send it back (off_style) if any stretch of more than 3 s has no cut or punch-in (karaoke captions change on every word, so they don't count), or if the captions are small or longer than three words.
- A frame jump is the point; a soft or cropped face is not. Send it back if a punch-in blurs the face or cuts it off.
- It still needs a hook in the first second, a face that stays in frame through the punch-ins, and a final line that pays off.
