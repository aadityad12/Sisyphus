# One idea, plain talking head

Clean and unfussy: one strong idea told in the speaker's own words, few cuts, plain readable captions, and nothing on screen that the viewer has to work out. The lo-fi, unpolished look that sits well beside phone-shot content.

- Aim for 20-45 s unless the brief says otherwise. Keep 1-3 segments of one continuous thought; cut only dead air or a tangent.
- The hook text states the idea plainly, in under eight words, or is empty if the first spoken line already does it.
- Captions: plain, caption_words 4-6, caption_size small, caption_color white, caption_position middle, caption_caps false.
- Framing: crop with track_face true for a talking head; fit when a slide or screen carries the point.
- No punch-ins (punch 1.0), color_filter none, speed 1.0.
- End when the thought completes.
