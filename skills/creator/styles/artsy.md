# Artsy

Mood over information: a slower, deliberate rhythm, room to breathe, a graded look, sparse captions, and an opening line chosen because it's intriguing, not because it explains.

- Open on an evocative or philosophical line, even if it raises a question the clip only half answers. Hook text is short and lowercase, or empty.
- Let it breathe: 2-4 longer segments. Keep a pause before the key line instead of cutting it.
- Captions: plain, caption_words 2-3, caption_size small, caption_color white, caption_position upper or middle, caption_caps false. caption_style none is allowed when the speech is clear.
- Framing: crop with an off-centre composition (crop_x 0.35-0.65 around the face) or fit for a wide shot; zoom 1.2-1.6 for an intimate close-up.
- color_filter bw or film; speed 0.9-1.0. No punch-ins.
- End on a line that lingers.
