# Podcast exchange clip

A good clip in this style is one complete exchange from a conversation that makes sense on its own, with the person talking on screen.

- Longer clips and calm cuts at speaker turns are right here; don't call them slow unless there is dead air.
- Send it back if the face on screen belongs to someone who isn't talking for more than a moment, or if the clip needs context from outside it to make sense.
- Send it back if it ends mid-exchange: the question needs its answer.
- Send it back (off_style) for brain-rot treatment: rapid punch-ins, all-caps one- or two-word captions, a heavy colour filter.
