# Brain rot

A good brain-rot clip never lets attention drop: something changes every one to three seconds, and the captions are big enough to read with the sound off.

- Fast cuts, jump zooms and frantic pacing are the point here: do not send a brain-rot clip back for pacing that is too fast.
- Send it back (off_style) if any stretch of more than 4 s has no cut, punch-in or caption change, or if the captions are small or long.
- It still needs a hook in the first second, a face that stays in frame through the punch-ins, and a final line that pays off.
