# Brain rot jump cuts

Maximum stimulation for a scrolling thumb: the most surprising line first, cuts every one and a half to three seconds, big shouty captions two or three words at a time, punch-ins on every cut, and no breathing room anywhere.

- Aim for 15-30 s unless the brief says otherwise. Open on the single most surprising or outrageous line in the source, even if it comes from the middle. The hook text is short, all caps in spirit, and promises chaos ("HE SAID WHAT").
- Cut hard and often: 10-20 segments of 1.5-3 s, with every pause, "um" and run-up dropped. Reordering for punch is fine; changing what someone meant is not.
- Captions: karaoke, caption_words 2-3, caption_size medium, caption_caps true, caption_color yellow or white, caption_position middle. One word at a time flashes by too fast to read.
- Punch in: alternate punch between 1.0 and 1.12-1.20 from one segment to the next, so the frame jumps on each cut. Bigger punches upscale the face until it goes soft.
- Framing: crop, track_face true. color_filter vivid; speed 1.1-1.25 (past 1.25 speech gets hard to follow).
- Still end on a payoff line, ideally one that flows back into the opening: brain rot is fast, not broken.
