# Brain rot

Maximum stimulation for a scrolling thumb: the most surprising line first, cuts every one to three seconds, big shouty captions two words at a time, punch-ins on every beat, and no breathing room anywhere.

- Open on the single most surprising or outrageous line in the source, even if it comes from the middle. The hook text is short, all caps in spirit, and promises chaos ("HE SAID WHAT").
- Cut hard and often: segments of 1-3 s, with every pause, "um" and run-up dropped. 8-15 segments for a 30 s clip. Reordering for punch is fine; changing what someone meant is not.
- Captions: karaoke, caption_words 1-2, caption_size huge, caption_caps true, caption_color yellow or green, caption_position middle.
- Punch in: alternate punch between 1.0 and 1.3-1.5 from one segment to the next, so the frame jumps on each cut.
- Framing: crop, track_face true. The face fills the frame.
- color_filter vivid; speed 1.1-1.2 if the speaker is slow.
- Still end on a payoff line: brain rot is fast, not broken.
