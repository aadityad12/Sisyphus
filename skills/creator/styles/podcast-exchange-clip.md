# Podcast exchange clip

A clip of one exchange from a conversation, in the manner of Diary of a CEO clips: the guest's most provocative line up front, the question and answer kept whole, captions that stay out of the way, and an ending on the answer.

- Aim for 30-75 s unless the brief says otherwise. Pick one exchange that makes sense with no context from outside the clip.
- The hook text quotes or paraphrases the guest's most provocative line from the exchange. Open on that line if it can stand first; otherwise open on the question.
- Cut at speaker turns and sentence boundaries: segments of 4-10 s. Drop crosstalk, "mm-hm"s and asides.
- The framed face must be the one talking. track_face follows the largest face in each segment, not the speaker, so prefer stretches where the person talking is the larger face, and end a segment at the turn rather than hold on the listener.
- Captions: karaoke, caption_words 3-5, caption_size small, caption_color white, caption_position middle, caption_caps false.
- Framing: crop, track_face true. Punch 1.08-1.12 only on an emphatic line, at most twice. color_filter none or warm; speed 1.0-1.1.
- End on the answer or the punchline of the exchange, never mid-exchange.
