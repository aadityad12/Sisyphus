# Hormozi-style bold captions

The big-caption talking-head look popularised by Alex Hormozi: a claim in the first second, heavy uppercase captions two or three words at a time with the spoken word lit up, every digression cut, and a small punch-in every few seconds.

- Aim for 20-45 s unless the brief says otherwise. Open on the most compelling statement in the source; the hook text is that claim in 3-6 words.
- Cut every digression, pause and run-up: 6-12 segments of 2-4 s each. Reordering for punch is fine; changing what someone meant is not.
- Something visible changes every 3-5 s: alternate punch between 1.0 and 1.10-1.15 from one segment to the next.
- Captions: karaoke, caption_words 2-3, caption_size medium, caption_color white (the spoken word turns yellow), caption_position middle, caption_caps true.
- Framing: crop, track_face true. color_filter none or vivid; speed 1.0-1.1.
- End on the punchline, not on a setup or a call to action.
