# Polished

Broadcast quality: a clean cold open, tight but invisible cuts on sentence boundaries, steady framing, captions that look designed, and an ending that lands like a closing line.

- Open on a line that sets up the question the clip answers; the hook text frames it as a headline in sentence case.
- Cut on sentence boundaries only, so no edit is noticeable. 3-6 segments; trim every filler word and false start.
- Captions: karaoke, caption_words 3-4, caption_size large, caption_color white, caption_position lower, caption_caps false.
- Framing: one consistent framing for the whole clip. split when a slide matters and the speaker is beside it; otherwise crop with track_face true.
- At most one punch-in (punch 1.15), on the clip's key line. color_filter warm or none; speed 1.0.
- End on the strongest concluding sentence in the source, never on a setup.
