# Broadcast polish, invisible cuts

A good clip in this style looks professionally produced: you can't see the cuts, the framing never jumps, captions are consistent, and it ends on a closing line.

- Send it back for any visible edit: a cut mid-word or mid-sentence, a jump in framing between segments, a caption flicker.
- Send it back (off_style) for anything that looks amateur or gimmicky: more than one punch-in, a strong colour filter, all-caps or oversized captions.
- The ending matters most here: it must land as a conclusion, not a setup.
