# One idea, plain talking head

A good clip in this style makes one idea easy to follow: few cuts, plain captions, nothing distracting.

- Long takes and calm pacing are right here; don't call them slow unless there is real dead air.
- Send it back (off_style) for flashy treatment: any punch-in, a colour filter, captions larger than small, or one- or two-word captions.
- The idea must be clear by the end without the brief.
