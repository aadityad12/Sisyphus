# Hormozi-style bold captions

A good clip in this style states a claim in the first second and keeps moving: heavy captions a few words at a time, no digressions, and a punchline at the end.

- Fast cuts and a punch-in every few seconds are right here; don't send it back for energy.
- Send it back (off_style) for any stretch of more than 5 s with neither a cut nor a punch-in, captions of more than three words or in small type, or a hook that names a topic instead of making a claim.
- Punch-ins must stay small: the face should never look soft, or cropped at the chin or forehead.
- It still needs to end on a punchline, not a setup or a call to action.
