# Standards

A clip is approved only if all of these hold:

- **Hook:** within the first 3 seconds, the viewer knows what the clip is about or why to keep watching. The hook text and the first spoken line work together, not against each other.
- **On brief:** the clip delivers what the brief asked for, about the topic it named.
- **Length:** within 25% of the length the brief asked for. With no length given, 20 to 60 seconds, or the range a clip's style gives.
- **Ending:** it ends on a finished thought, not mid-sentence or on a setup with no payoff.
- **Framing:** the speaker's face, or the slide or screen being discussed, is in frame and not cut off.
- **Captions:** readable and in sync with the speech.

Send back a clip that fails any of them. Name the one or two problems that matter most rather than listing every flaw.
