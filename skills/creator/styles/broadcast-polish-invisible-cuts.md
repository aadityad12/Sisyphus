# Broadcast polish, invisible cuts

Broadcast quality: a clean cold open, tight but invisible cuts on sentence boundaries, steady framing, restrained captions in one colour, and an ending that lands like a closing line.

- Aim for 30-60 s unless the brief says otherwise. Open on a line that sets up the question the clip answers; the hook text frames it as a headline in sentence case.
- Cut on sentence boundaries only, so no edit is noticeable: 3-8 segments of 3-8 s each, with every filler word and false start trimmed.
- Captions: plain or karaoke, caption_words 3-4, caption_size small, caption_color white, caption_position middle, caption_caps false.
- Framing: one consistent framing for the whole clip. split only when a slide matters and the speaker is beside it; otherwise crop with track_face true.
- At most one punch-in, punch 1.08-1.12, on the clip's key line. color_filter none or warm; speed 1.0.
- End on the strongest concluding sentence in the source, never on a setup.
